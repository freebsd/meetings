# 2026-07-10 - Core.14 Meeting

## Secretary Role

The role of core and potentially project secretary was discussed.
Similar discussion points were covered in the previous core.13
meeting minutes (2026-05-18).  The scope of the role (core, or project
wide) was discussed along with some larger responsibilities befitting
a more permanent, full time funded role.

Existing examples from other non-profits and FreeBSD working groups
were discussed.  The next steps include finalising the scope, roles/
responsibilities and putting together a job proposal together
to seek both candidates and suitable funding from the FreeBSD Foundation.

## Cyber Resilience Act (CRA)

The FreeBSD Foundation approached core.13 about the reporting and support
required by the upcoming European Cyber Resilience Act (CRA).  core.13 /
core.14 and the FreeBSD Foundation are continuing discussions to finalise
these plans and will announce something shortly.

For more information about the CRA and the reporting / support requirements
by organisations, please see
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cyber-resilience-act .
